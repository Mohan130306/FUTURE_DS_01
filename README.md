# Sales Data Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

##  About This Project
This project is a sales data analysis dashboard created using **Power BI**.  
The main aim of this project is to understand business sales performance and answer important questions such as which products sell the most, how sales change over time, and which regions generate higher revenue.

This project was completed as part of an **internship task** to practice real-world data analysis skills.

---

##  Objectives of the Project
- Analyze sales data to understand business performance  
- Identify top revenue-generating products  
- Study sales trends over time  
- Find high-performing regions  
- Provide simple business insights and recommendations  

---

##  Tools Used
- **Power BI** – for data visualization and dashboard creation  
- **Excel / CSV** – as the data source  

---

##  Dashboard Overview
The Power BI dashboard includes:

- **KPI Cards**
  - Total Revenue
  - Total Quantity Sold
  - Total Customers
  - Total Orders

- **Top 10 Products by Revenue**
  - Shows products that generate the highest revenue

- **Revenue Trend Over Time**
  - Displays how sales change over different time periods

- **Revenue by Country**
  - Highlights countries that contribute the most to total revenue

- **Quantity vs Revenue Scatter Chart**
  - Compares product sales volume with revenue

- **Insights and Recommendations**
  - Written observations and suggestions for business improvement

---

##  Key Insights
- A small number of products contribute a large portion of total revenue.
- The United Kingdom is the highest revenue-generating country.
- Some products sell in high quantity but generate less revenue.
- Sales performance varies over time, showing possible seasonal patterns.

---

##  Recommendations
- Focus more on top-performing products to increase revenue.
- Improve marketing strategies in high-performing regions.
- Review pricing for products with high sales volume but low revenue.
- Use sales trends to plan inventory and promotions better.
